Theodotos-Alexandreus Theodotos-Alexandreus: Over and over again over the years of my life a disturbing thought paid a visit to my aging soul and quietly wispered to me that it looks like our whole civilization gradually became an enterprise of building "The Machine" that protects us from the hostilities of Nature but deprives us of its serendipity too. The Machine houses and nurtures us from the day we are born...
